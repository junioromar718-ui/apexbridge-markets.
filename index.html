Account concepts

Choose the structure that fits.

Account names below are planning concepts—not available accounts or promises of execution conditions.

CONCEPT 01

Zero-Spread

A possible commission-based account structure, if supported by future providers and permitted by regulation.

  • Potentially tight quoted spreads
  • Commission and total-cost disclosure
  • Risk tools and account information
CONCEPT 02

Low-Spread

A possible spread-based account structure with pricing and fees disclosed before launch.

  • Spread-based pricing concept
  • Clear terms and fee information
  • Risk disclosures and support
Important: “Zero spread” does not mean zero trading cost. Commission, slippage, financing, and other charges may apply. No account terms have been set or verified.

Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Can I open a trading account here?

No. This is a website prototype only. Account registration and live trading are not enabled.

Is ApexBridge Markets licensed?

This concept does not claim that the proposed company is licensed. Before operating, the business must confirm the applicable authorisations with the relevant regulators.

Are the spreads and fees confirmed?

No. The zero-spread and low-spread account types are concepts. Actual pricing, commissions, and other costs would need to be agreed with providers and disclosed accurately.
